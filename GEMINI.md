# Aegis Project Mandates: Engineering Excellence

This document supersedes all general instructions. All contributions, documentation, and blog posts must adhere to these standards to ensure the project signals world-class engineering mastery.

## 1. Tone and Voice
*   **Target Audience:** CTOs, Directors of Engineering, and Principal Architects.
*   **Tone:** Objective, clinical, and data-driven.
*   **Inspiration:** Netflix Technology Blog, Uber Engineering, Cloudflare Blog.
*   **Prohibited Jargon (AI Slop):** 
    *   NO "Flex", "Aha!", "Magic", "Staff-level", "War Story", "Mind-blowing", "Game-changer".
    *   NO dramatic adjectives or conversational filler.
    *   NO marketing-speak or hype.

## 2. Technical Integrity
*   **Show, Don't Tell:** Never claim a system is "fast" or "efficient." Provide the exact millisecond latency, memory usage in MB, and throughput metrics.
*   **Code-First Explanation:** Use surgical code snippets to explain architectural pivots.
*   **Failure as Fact:** Document technical fractures (bugs) as objective "Case Studies" or "System Post-Mortems." Focus on the Root Cause and the Resolution.

## 3. Blog Structure (Phase 3 and beyond)
*   **Title:** Descriptive and impact-focused.
*   **Intro:** Contextualize the problem within the distributed system.
*   **Sections:** Problem -> Investigation -> Data/Metrics -> Resolution.
*   **Conclusion:** Summary of the architectural state.
