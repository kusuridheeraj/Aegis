# Global Engineering Mandates: Excellence and Integrity

These mandates apply to all projects, documentation, and technical communications. The goal is to ensure all output signals world-class engineering mastery and technical maturity.

## 1. Professional Tone and Voice
*   **Target Audience:** CTOs, Directors of Engineering, and Principal Architects.
*   **Tone:** Objective, clinical, and data-driven. Model after industry-standard engineering blogs (e.g., Netflix, Uber, Cloudflare).
*   **Prohibited Jargon:** Never use conversational fluff or buzzwords such as "Flex," "Aha!", "Magic," "Staff-level," "War Story," "Mind-blowing," or "Game-changer."
*   **Clarity:** Eliminate dramatic adjectives and marketing-speak. Use precise technical terminology.

## 2. Technical Integrity
*   **Show, Don't Tell:** Provide exact metrics (latency in ms, throughput in req/s, memory in MB). Never rely on vague claims of "efficiency."
*   **Code-First:** Use surgical code snippets to explain implementation details and architectural pivots.
*   **Incident Post-Mortems:** Document system fractures or edge cases as objective technical analysis. Focus on the Root Cause, the Investigation, and the verifiable Resolution.

## 3. Communication Standards
*   **Conciseness:** Be direct and high-signal. Avoid preambles or conversational filler.
*   **Accuracy:** Prioritize empirical evidence over assumptions. Fulfill requests thoroughly while maintaining idiomatic quality and system safety.
